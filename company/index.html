HuanYu Connector Technology Co., Ltd (est. 2016) builds the terminals on this site in Jinan, Shandong — a wholly-owned subsidiary of HuanYu Technology Group, working alongside Shandong Universal Cables (est. 2000) on the same campus. Tooling, stamping and inspection are shared works: one yard, two factories.

The group supplies global OEMs and listed vehicle makers (customer names shared on request). On this site, every reel ships only after 100% optical inspection on self-built vision lines.

The tool room,
the floor.

Molds are designed, cut and finished in our own tool room; stamping, plating and 100% optical inspection all happen on the Jinan campus. Stamping runs on imported vertical high-speed presses — 500–1,800 strokes a minute.
