What a cross means

A HuanYu cross-reference says: this HuanYu terminal is the dimensional and functional equivalent of the named OEM part. It is not the OEM part and never claimed to be one.

How a cross is validated

Side-by-side measurement of the stamped part against the OEM drawing — overall envelope, crimp barrel profile, wire-range windows, material thickness and plating class. A cross is published only when the crimp profile matches; "close but not equal" parts are listed without a cross.

What we send you

On request: a dimension report (measured vs drawing), crimp cross-sections, and a sample reel for your own pull-and-section tests. Ask in the RFQ and the reply carries it.

When not to trust any cross

Sealed systems. A terminal cross inside a connector housing also depends on the seal, the housing and the secondary lock — for sealed applications send us the mating system, not just the terminal number.

Want a cross we do not publish yet? Paste the OEM number into the RFQ — unlisted crosses are answered with the same dimension evidence when the tooling exists, and a straight "no" when it does not.
