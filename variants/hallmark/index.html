HuanYu Connector Technology Co., Ltd (est. 2016) builds the terminals on this site in Jinan, Shandong, a wholly-owned subsidiary of HuanYu Technology Group working alongside Shandong Universal Cables (est. 1993) on the same campus. Tooling, plating and inspection are shared works: one yard, two factories.

The group supplies global OEMs and listed vehicle makers, customer names shared on request. On this site every reel ships only after 100% optical inspection on self-built vision lines.

The works

Molds are designed, cut and finished in our own tool room; stamping, plating and 100% optical inspection all happen on the Jinan campus. The photographs are from the group 2021 brochure; fresh shop-floor photography is being commissioned.
