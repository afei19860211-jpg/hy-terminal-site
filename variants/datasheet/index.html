HuanYu Connector Technology Co., Ltd (est. 2016) builds the terminals on this site in Jinan, Shandong — a wholly-owned subsidiary of HuanYu Technology Group, working alongside Shandong Universal Cables (est. 1993) on the same campus. Tooling, plating and inspection are shared works: one yard, two factories.

The group supplies global OEMs and listed vehicle makers (customer names shared on request). 30 patents filed, 28 granted. On this site, every reel ships only after 100% optical inspection on self-built vision lines.

Factory, plainly

Molds are designed, cut and finished in our own tool room; stamping, plating and 100% optical inspection all happen on the Jinan campus. Photographs below are from the group's 2021 brochure — fresh shop-floor photography is being commissioned.
